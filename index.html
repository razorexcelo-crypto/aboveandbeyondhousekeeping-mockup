Common Questions

Before you book

The questions we hear most from new clients across Berks County.

Do I need to provide any cleaning supplies?+

No, we arrive with everything needed, including professional-grade products and a cleaning kit (towels, mop, bucket) reserved just for your home.

Am I locked into a contract?+

Not for one-time cleanings. Recurring plans are flexible and can be adjusted, paused, or cancelled as your needs change.

What if I'm not happy with a cleaning?+

Every visit is reviewed by our Quality Control team. If something's missed, let us know within 24 hours and we'll make it right.

Will I have the same cleaner every time?+

Yes. Once you're matched with a specialist, they're yours for every future visit, barring vacation or illness.

Do I need to be home for my cleaning?+

No. We just need a way to get in at the scheduled time. If you'd rather be home, that's fine too, we'll work around you.

What if something gets damaged during a cleaning?+

Accidents are rare, but if something is ever damaged we'll let you know right away. Verified items are covered up to $100 or replacement cost.

Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?+

Yes, every specialist is fully insured and vetted before they're ever sent to a client's home.

How do I get a quote?+

Call, text, or fill out the form below. We typically follow up the same day, usually within the hour.

Still have questions?
